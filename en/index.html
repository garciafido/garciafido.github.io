Fernando Daniel (Fido) García

Over forty years building software, in science and in business.

ORCID · Google Scholar · Scopus · LinkedIn · Bandcamp · CIMA · Agilmind · Profile (PDF)

Computer scientist with a career in two worlds: scientific research, at CONICET (Argentina’s National Scientific and Technical Research Council) since 1984, where he has developed software for mathematics, ecology, energy and the atmospheric sciences; and business, where he founded technology companies for different markets. Three traits recur throughout: an interest in new technologies as soon as they appear, building his own tools, and carrying ideas from one field to another.

Science

  • Principal Professional at CONICET since 1984; currently at CIMA-IFAECI.
  • Development of software and solutions for research in mathematics, ecology, nuclear energy and the atmospheric sciences.

Business

  • Co-founder of technology companies since 1993; since 2006, CTO and co-founder of Agilmind SRL.
  • AI systems architecture since Axe Music Star (2009); today, model orchestration in AgilPost and AgilBank.
  • Electronic signature (AgilSign), management systems and Jupiter, a platform for the music industry; author of JDX.

Music

  • Singer: folk music since the age of 12, founder of Inti Huasi; member of the Grupo de Experimentación Vocal (GEV).
  • Arrangements for the Fundación San Telmo choir.
  • Music for theatre (El tuerto es rey, 1987; Kaspar, 1991; Digidramamancia, 2005; Vanya en miniatura, 2025).
  • Solo album Ronda (2015).

Own tools. The languages Augur (expert systems), ShowMe (multimedia presentations) and Haiku (full-stack web applications); his own inference engine for expert systems (1989) and an LR/LALR parser generator (1990).

Education. Programador Superior (computer programming degree), Universidad Nacional de San Luis (1983). Genetic Algorithms in Search, Optimization, and Machine Learning, University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign, with David E. Goldberg (2000).
