Fernando Daniel García

Over forty years building software, in science and in business.

ORCID · Google Scholar · Scopus · LinkedIn · Bandcamp · Agilmind · Profile (PDF)

Profile

Computer scientist with a career in two worlds: scientific research, at CONICET (Argentina’s National Scientific and Technical Research Council) since 1984, where he has developed software for mathematics, ecology, energy and the atmospheric sciences; and business, where he founded technology companies for different markets. Three traits recur throughout: an interest in new technologies as soon as they appear, building his own tools, and carrying ideas from one field to another.

Science

At CONICET since 1984, as Principal Professional, developing software and technological solutions for research teams. Currently at the Centro de Investigaciones del Mar y la Atmósfera (CIMA, CONICET-UBA) and, since 2016, at IFAECI (IRL 3351, CNRS-CONICET-IRD-UBA).

Fields

  • Mathematics: symbolic artificial intelligence (symbolic differentiation in Prolog) and network reliability.
  • Ecology: population dynamics models and expert systems for natural resource management, with his own inference engine.
  • Nuclear energy (CNEA, 1987): a rule-based prototype to detect abnormal events in the RA3 reactor.
  • Atmospheric sciences (CIMA-IFAECI): satellite (GOES-16) and radar data, bots that collect data from automatic weather stations, community-based flood early warning, and a severe weather event registry for five countries (JICA–SMN).

Selected projects

  • Community-based flood early warning systems (Anticipando la Crecida, IMPACT.AR).
  • Severe weather event registry for five countries (PREVENIR, JICA–SMN).
  • CIMA-GOES, a library for bulk download and processing of GOES-16 satellite data.
  • Automated real-time collection of weather station data (RELAMPAGO field campaign; dataset published at NCAR).
  • CIMAPS, a web platform for georeferenced visualization.

Business

Co-founder and CTO of XG Tecnología, García & Strauss, FT WAVE and Agilmind; consultant at Borland Argentina.

  • XG Tecnología (1993–1995): interactive presentations and kiosks with games for Ford, Volkswagen, Toyota, Sancor and Telecom, among others, built on his own engine on top of the hardware manufacturers’ libraries rather than the “multimedia” tools of the time.
  • García & Strauss (1995–2004): games and interaction built into business applications, such as Car One’s self-service information terminals.
  • FT WAVE (2000–2002): design of an online system for distributing new music releases to radio stations across Argentina over dial-up connections, with its own digital music player, hired by Sony Music, EMI Music, Warner Music, BMG, Universal Music and Leader Music.
  • Borland Argentina (2001–2006): consultant; development of a rapid application development (RAD) framework for Delphi and design of multi-tier architectures.
  • Agilmind (2009): an interactive game for an Axe product that ran on a desktop computer’s webcam: adaptive music, computer vision, genetic algorithms and real-time non-linear video.
  • Agilmind today: artificial intelligence for companies operating in Vaca Muerta, with AgilFace (facial recognition) and AgilPost (intelligent posting of accounting documents), and Jupiter, a platform for the music industry and copyright.

Music

He has sung in folk groups since the age of 12 (among them Inti Huasi, which he founded), was a member of the Grupo de Experimentación Vocal (GEV), wrote arrangements for the Fundación San Telmo choir and has composed music for theatre, such as Vanya en miniatura (2025). Solo album: Ronda (2015). In 2005 he developed the software for Digidramamancia, which generated the script and the score in real time from the audience’s fingerprints, and was responsible for its metacomposition and musical direction. Registered as author, performer and phonogram producer with SADAIC, AADI and CAPIF. Music at https://fidogarcia.bandcamp.com.

Own tools

The languages Augur (expert systems), ShowMe (multimedia presentations) and Haiku (full-stack web applications); his own inference engine for expert systems (1989) and an LR/LALR parser generator (1990).

Education

Programador Superior (computer programming degree), Universidad Nacional de San Luis (1983). Genetic Algorithms, University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign, with David E. Goldberg (2000).
