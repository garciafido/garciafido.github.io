Fernando Daniel (Fido) García

Music

He has sung in folk groups since the age of 12 (among them Inti Huasi, which he founded), was a member of the Grupo de Experimentación Vocal (GEV), wrote arrangements for the Fundación San Telmo choir and has composed music for theatre, such as Vanya en miniatura (2025). Solo album: Ronda (2015). In 2005 he developed the software for Digidramamancia, which generated the script and the score in real time from the audience’s fingerprints, and was responsible for its metacomposition and musical direction. Registered as author, performer and phonogram producer with SADAIC, AADI and CAPIF. Music at https://fidogarcia.bandcamp.com.
