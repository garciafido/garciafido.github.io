Fernando Daniel (Fido) García

Music

He has sung in folk groups since the age of 12 (among them Inti Huasi, which he founded), was a member of the Grupo de Experimentación Vocal (GEV), wrote arrangements for the Fundación San Telmo choir and has composed music for theatre, such as Vanya en miniatura (2025), directed by José María López (Kusmis Teatro); earlier, for El tuerto es rey by Carlos Fuentes (1987) and Kaspar by Peter Handke (1991), both directed by Juan Polito. Solo album: Ronda (2015). In 2005 he developed the software for Digidramamancia, which generated the script and the score in real time from the audience’s fingerprints, and was responsible for its metacomposition and musical direction; the play was also directed by José María López. Registered as author, performer and phonogram producer with SADAIC, AADI and CAPIF. Music at https://fidogarcia.bandcamp.com.
