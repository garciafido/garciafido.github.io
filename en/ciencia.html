Fernando Daniel (Fido) García

Science

At CONICET since 1984, as Principal Professional, developing software and technological solutions for research teams. Currently at the Centro de Investigaciones del Mar y la Atmósfera (CIMA, CONICET-UBA) and, since 2016, at IFAECI (IRL 3351, CNRS-CONICET-IRD-UBA).

Full academic CV, in PDF

Fields

  • Mathematics: symbolic artificial intelligence (symbolic differentiation in Prolog) and network reliability.
  • Ecology: population dynamics models and expert systems for natural resource management, with his own inference engine.
  • Nuclear energy (CNEA, 1987): a rule-based prototype to detect abnormal events in the RA3 reactor.
  • Atmospheric sciences (CIMA-IFAECI): satellite (GOES-16) and radar data, bots that collect data from automatic weather stations, community-based flood early warning, and a severe weather event registry for five countries (JICA–SMN).

Selected projects

  • Community-based flood early warning systems (Anticipando la Crecida, IMPACT.AR).
  • Severe weather event registry for five countries (PREVENIR, JICA–SMN).
  • CIMA-GOES, a library for bulk download and processing of GOES-16 satellite data.
  • Automated real-time collection of weather station data (RELAMPAGO field campaign; dataset published at NCAR).
  • CIMAPS, a web platform for georeferenced visualization.
